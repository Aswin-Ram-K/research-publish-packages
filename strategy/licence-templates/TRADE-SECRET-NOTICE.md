## The mechanisms are trade secrets

The designs, mechanisms, internals and implementation details contained in this repository are
maintained as **trade secrets and confidential information**. They are deliberately not published.
A trade secret survives only while it is actually kept secret, so the obligations below are
operative, not decorative.

## Hygiene obligations (the author must actually observe these)

Trade-secret status is a practice, not a label. Observing these is what preserves it:

1. **Restrict access.** Keep the repository private. Grant access to named persons only, and
   remove access when it is no longer needed. Do not publish, mirror, fork publicly, or attach
   the repository to any public service.
2. **Mark material confidential.** Keep this notice and the `LICENSE` in the tree at all times.
   Mark files and excerpts that are shared outside the repository with a confidentiality legend.
3. **Written agreement before disclosure.** Before showing any covered mechanism to anyone outside
   the author's own control — a collaborator, a reviewer, a contractor, a prospective commercial
   licensee — have a **written agreement in place first** (for example a confidentiality / NDA
   agreement, or a written licence that expressly covers the disclosure). Do not disclose first and
   paper it later.
4. **No publication of any covered mechanism.** Do not publish, blog, post, submit, present, or
   otherwise disclose a covered mechanism before it has been deliberately extracted and released
   as a public piece. A single public disclosure of a mechanism ends its trade-secret status for
   that mechanism.
5. **Release by extraction, deliberately.** Public release happens only through the separate
   step of extracting a piece into its own repository and giving that piece its own licence
   (PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 + `COMMERCIAL.md` + `CONTRIBUTING.md`). The main repository is
   never made public as a whole.
