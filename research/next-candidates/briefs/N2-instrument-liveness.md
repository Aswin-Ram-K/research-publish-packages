# N2 — An instrument that cannot say yes: a defect taxonomy and pre-flight for LLM measurement harnesses

**Thesis in one sentence.** Every null in this workspace's serving/quality record came from an instrument that could not have reported a positive (or could not say what its zero meant), noticed only by a later verifier; the paper is that taxonomy plus a falsifiable pre-flight with one red→green demonstration per class.

**Why it is new.** None of the four papers carries a harness-liveness protocol: grep over `papers/*/MANUSCRIPT.md` and `papers/*/CLAIMS.md` for `liveness`, `pre-flight`, `verified_success`, `unstamped` returns zero hits (`n_eff` appears only inside `interaction_effect`). Nearest claim id: **F4 seeded-copy positive controls** (`papers/encoder-crossover-190/CLAIMS.md:95`, fired 13/44/57) — that proves one leak lint can fire, not that the pipeline can say yes; `papers/context-cache/MANUSCRIPT.md:247` calls accuracy "the wrong instrument", and C55/C57 test only whether the *server* reports cache counters. Ours gates the whole path — emitter, fixture, selector, admission, ledger — before a null counts. Borrowed and to be cited, not claimed: `docs/EXTERNAL-EVAL-PRACTICE.md` §2.1/#9 probe-validated graders, Wilson CIs, McNemar, canary #11. **Abstract draft.** An LLM measurement harness that can only report failure cannot support a null: its "no difference" may be an instrument that could never have said yes. We present a defect taxonomy and a falsifiable pre-flight protocol, evidenced entirely by one workspace's own failed serving and quality measurements. The classes: an exact-match scorer that rejects every fixture-conforming log (positive control `PATH-CANNOT-SAY-YES`; four negative controls indistinguishable from it); a fixture-priced treatment unreachable within the horizon (8 of 16 cells, zero marker rows); replicate collapse in a deduplication key (288 rows analysed as 96; n_eff 26.14 against a floor of 60); admission of unstamped cells; a billing projection that killed two of its own cells; a zero with four meanings; a denominator that vanishes unnoticed; a frozen ledger that refuses every launch. Each class carries a red and a green demonstration, all reproducible offline at zero endpoint requests. We separate the protocol from established evaluation practice and scope the result as a single-workspace case series.
