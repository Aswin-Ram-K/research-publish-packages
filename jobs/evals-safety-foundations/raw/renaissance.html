A brighter future for all through science, technology, and innovation

Our mission is to fuel a 21st century renaissance by increasing the ambition of philanthropists, scientists, and innovators.

Our Story ->

We build time-bound, thesis-driven philanthropic funds, enabling philanthropists and foundations to support ideas that advance entire fields forward, without building large in-house teams of experts. 

Explore Our Work ->

Our Impact

We create impact by catalyzing philanthropic dollars for science, technology and innovation and inspiring others to take action. 

In our first two years, we have catalyzed $533M in philanthropic capital behind our mission.

$265M

unlocked for other organizations through matchmaking

$268M

directly raised for our programs and funds
