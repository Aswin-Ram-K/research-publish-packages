# N1 — Alignment, not size: what an append actually costs a prefix cache, and the two-token fix

**Thesis in one sentence.** On a prefix-cached chat server with cache block size *B*, appending *L* tokens to a primed prefix of *P1* tokens re-bills `(P1 mod B) + L` — a tail set by the history's alignment residue and independent of the payload — and at residue 0 or 1 the cache stops paying altogether, so an append that should cost 13 tokens is charged the whole prefix on one instrument and one block plus tail on another; padding the history by two tokens before appending converts that class change into a tail.

**Why it is new.** The in-draft paper's nearest neighbours are **C40/C41** (`papers/context-cache/MANUSCRIPT.md` §9: immediate-repeat misses only at `L ≡ 0 (mod 64)`, 5/5 vs 0/15, Fisher p=3.06e-07; second identical repeat hits at `64·floor((L−1)/64)`, 10/10), **C55–C57** (§9a: per-stack grid — SGLang 64; vLLM resolved 1,088 / 1,056 / 2,096), and **§8a** (aligning to the grid costs more than it recovers: pad 94.89 mean vs 74.06 recovered). None of them states the append cost law, vLLM's `+B` term, or a rule that fires *before* the append; `papers/context-cache/` mentions the law only in fold/compilation plumbing (C76 is the framing identity `L_app = n_payload + 12`, not the cost law). The contribution is a **closed-form per-append cost law with an exact fit, a discontinuity class, a cross-engine tax ratio, and a client-side intervention** — and, because the two instruments disagree at the discontinuity, a measurement point about what a cache "hit" is.

Concessions, from the law's own §8: the **mechanism** of the cliff is unexplained and must be reported as measured; only **one append onto a primed prefix** is swept, so multi-append conversation cost is extrapolation; vLLM's numbers rest on a single server-side counter (`vllm:prompt_tokens_cached_total`, both cache counters count tokens on this build), so `+B` wants independent re-derivation; no residue-1 *warm* SGLang cell exists anywhere. **Contradictions a reviewer will find.**
1. **The discontinuity's magnitude is instrument-dependent, and the instruments do not agree on absence.** `experiments/ptr-engine/RECORD.md` §5 **C1**: a second instrument (`/generate` `meta_info.cached_tokens`) reads **cached = 8,000** at P1=8064 and bills `64 + L`, calling `LAW.md`'s 8,013 a ~5.2× overstatement; `usage.prompt_tokens_details` reads `null` at the same length and bills `P1 + L`. `RECORD.md:114` records the 5.2× as first withdrawn and then **UNDECIDABLE** — the rows fit both residue-0 readings (`orchestration/units/RECON-C1.md`). So the paper may assert: the tail law is exact; the cliff class is measured on both instruments; the *billed magnitude* is instrument-dependent; and the padding rule is positive under every reading (saves ≈64 tokens under the counter reading, ≈`P1 − r` under the null reading) [INFERENCE for the saving under the counter reading]. It may **not** assert absence as a cross-instrument fact, nor 8,013 as a universal magnitude.
2. **`L − 64` recovery is both true and not a contradiction — but only with the shape stated.** `MANUSCRIPT.md` §9 / C40 and `cells/E4`/`E4R` (45/45 vs 0/40, Fisher 3.46e-25; 40/40 at `L − 64` on reps 2–3) measure the **second byte-identical repeat**, which rep 1's own miss has already warmed. `LAW.md`'s 7,936 row is a **fresh cold prime** (nonce varies per rep), so residue 0 misses — which is what E4 rep 1 also shows (L=960 misses 5/5). Both hold. **Unsettled:** what an *append* costs after that recovery — the decisive cell is SGLang P1=8000 prime → identical resend → three appends, ≈9 requests. Do not settle it by assumption.
3. **C9 is superseded only as a bar to the law.** `RECORD.md` C9 records the earlier avalanche file as HARVESTED (one profile, 378 rows) and `FOLD-PROPOSAL.md` bucket (iii) forbids "any residue→avalanche law" because *residue is not separable from P1*. The constant-nonce design (fixed 10-token nonce, `P1` stepped tokenwise across a full block, plus the position control) separates them: phase predicts in-band (186/186) and is invariant across bands (11/11). C9 still governs HARVEST's 378-row cell table (`experiments/ptr-engine/ADOPTION.md:38`).
4. **§8a points the other way.** "Aligning costs more than it recovers" prices a 64-token *page-recovery* pad under one workload; the residue rule is a 2-token *cliff-avoidance* pad. Both can be true; the paper must draw the boundary or a reviewer will read them as contradictory.

**Do not carry:** the 5.2× as fact; absence as a cross-instrument claim; 8,013 as a universal re-bill; "recovery at `L − 64` is not reproduced" without the shape qualifier; `LAW.md:116`'s "four block indices"; the 0.211 s residue-2 warm TTFT as a general figure (one G6 pair, 8.49× on that pair; vLLM avalanche TTFT 1.234518 / 1.791722 / 2.214067 s at P1 6336 / 7392 / 8448).

**Venue and reader value.** MLSys, NSDI, or a serving/systems measurement track; strong as a short paper. Every inference-engine team and agent-runner author is one padding statement away from the cliff, and the rule is a few lines in a request builder. Reader value: a closed-form cost model with an exact residual, a class change, and an intervention cheaper than what it prevents.

**Kill criteria.** (a) The cliff does not reproduce on a second build or a fresh server → the law survives as a tail-cost model and the headline goes; reframe as a note. (b) Both instruments turn out to read the same counter with different null handling → the C1 dispute is a field-semantics footnote and the paper shrinks materially. (c) `+B` fails independent re-derivation → drop the 33× ratio rather than report it second-hand. (d) The paper cannot be written without asserting a mechanism → do not write it.

**Abstract draft.** Prefix caching is billed as reuse, but an append re-bills a tail whose size is set by arithmetic nobody instruments. Sweeping the primed length of a SGLang endpoint across a full 64-token block at fixed prompt content, we measure `uncached = (P1 mod 64) + L` with residual exactly zero in 186 of 186 rows, and payload-independence at every residue. At `P1 mod 64 ∈ {0,1}` the behaviour changes class: an append that adds 13 tokens is charged the whole prefix through one usage field and one block plus tail through another, and the identical repeat misses, so this is not rounding. The discontinuity is residue-based, not positional — 11 of 11 misses across six block indices. The same append on vLLM, whose resolved block is 1,056, pays `+B` on top of its tail: 33× the SGLang overhead. We also refute the reading that such boundaries produce the observed fetch-count sign flip; they account for at most 3.7 % of it. The client-side rule is two tokens: pad the history off residues 0 and 1 before appending.
