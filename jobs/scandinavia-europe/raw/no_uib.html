About UiB

The University of Bergen

The University of Bergen is an international research university with more than 20,000 students. We have been conducting research, education and artistic development work in Bergen since 1946.

UiB in Europe

UiB collaborates closely with universities, research communities and decision-makers across Europe. We take part in European research projects and university alliances, apply for funding from the EU's framework programme, and contribute input to EU research and education policy.
