Nobel Prize in Physics awarded to researcher at IceCube

Congratulations to Francis Halzen, researcher at the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the University of Wisconsin–Madison, on winning the Nobel Prize in Physics! Halzen is also an honorary doctor at Uppsala University.

IceCube is one of the world’s most advanced observatories for studying neutrinos that reach us from outer space. Researchers at Uppsala University have been involved since the project’s inception and have made significant contributions to the research.
