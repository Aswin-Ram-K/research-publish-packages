Grants

Funding to advance the science of AI security and tackle the field’s most pressing questions.

Overview

Advancing AI safety and security through funding and collaborations.

Widespread AI adoption requires research breakthrough across AI safety and security.

We collaborate with leading researchers and institutions to identify promising projects and rapidly get them the funding they need.

To date, we have run three large-scale grants programmes, including the >£15 million Alignment Fund. You can see our priority research areas from past programmes below.

Our programmes are not currently accepting applications.

Past Programmes

Alignment Project

Applications are now closed.

The Alignment Project supports research to prevent advanced AI systems from behaving dangerously—either intentionally or accidentally. It awards up to £1 million per project, enabling new theoretical and empirical work on developing robust alignment, oversight, and monitoring techniques.

View the Alignment Project

Challenge Fund

Applications are now closed.

The Challenge Fund supports research to understand and tackle potential risks from advanced AI. It awards up £200,000 per project, accelerating innovation across fields like safeguards, control, alignment, and societal resilience.

View Challenge Fund details

Overview

The Challenge Fund awards researchers grants of up to £200,000 per project to address pressing, unresolved questions in AI safety and security.

We are particularly interested in the research areas outlined here, but we also welcome proposals that explore other AI safety and security topics.

In addition to funding, AISI will provide some projects compute and opportunities to work with our experts.

What we are looking for

We are seeking applications that propose research addressing AISI's priority areas, or other promising research proposals to advance AI safety and security.

Proposals should demonstrate clear pathways to impact and offer research that would not otherwise occur without this funding.

Eligibility

The Challenge Fund is open to researchers based at eligible* UK and international academic institutions and non-profit organisations. More details on eligible applicants can be found in our Application Pack.

To learn more

  • The Applicantion Pack is the main resource explaining the fund and how to apply.
  • OurClarification Questions document answers frequently asked questions.
  • OurCost Guidance document gives recommendations on how much funding to ask for.
  • You can also watch our Applicant Webinars on Part 1 and Part 2. Part 1 gives an overview of the fund. Part 2 focuses on the research we're looking to fund and collaboration opportunities.

  • Shortlisted applicants will work with an AISI Research Sponsor to iterate and complete their full application.

    Timeline is typically 2 weeks, depending on the nature and scope of proposal and the level of iteration required.
  • Funding decision
  • Within 4 weeks of submission of full application
  • Project kick-off meeting
  • Within 2 weeks of signing the Grant Funding Agreement
  • Payment
  • ‍Payments will be made in monthly arrears, subject to submission of milestone-based reporting.
