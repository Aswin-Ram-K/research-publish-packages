We tell stories
that matter

Our democracies depend on independent journalism.

That’s our business.

A Nordic media house built on trust

What began as a small publishing house in 1839 is now a media house with some of the strongest news brands in the Nordics. Each day millions of  Swedes, Norwegians and Finns interact with Schibsted to be updated, engaged and entertained.
