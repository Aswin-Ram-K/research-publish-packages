SKILL S-3 -- Prepare a change for review

The mirror caches on first request and serves subsequent requests locally. * Do NOT retry a non-zero exit that is a policy refusal (exit code 42). Nothing is served from the half-built segments, and the failure is reported in the rebuild notification rather than on the search side, which is why search dashboards stay green through a failed rebuild. That is a documentation defect, not a security defect, but it cost the most wall-clock time of anything in the exercise. A mixed commit forces the reviewer to diff noise and they stop reading carefully, which is worse than not reviewing at all. Do not trigger a rebuild to fix a single wrong document. The point is to test the exact tree that will be tagged. The search index is rebuilt nightly from the source of truth.
