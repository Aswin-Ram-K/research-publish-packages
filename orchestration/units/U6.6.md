## Decisions / limits
* An in-root symlink alias is ALLOWED: realpath containment, not a ban on
  symlinks, is the boundary.
* The directory-symlink door is contained by the same predicate. `pathlib.rglob`
  on this interpreter does not traverse symlinked directories, so it is
  defence-in-depth there and is pinned at the predicate level.
* NOT TOCTOU-safe: validate-then-read is two steps, so a concurrent swap
  between `realpath` and `open` could still escape. Loopback-only plus local
  write access to a docket directory make that a low, unprioritised residual.
* `refresh_backlog`'s second `_read_text_capped` call site (roots: dockets_dir,
  cwd) was left alone: outside this unit's scope and not part of the bypass.
