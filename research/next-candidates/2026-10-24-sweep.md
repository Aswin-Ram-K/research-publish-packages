*Claim:* the cost of a context wall is a window decision, not a boundary event; the hybrid
(append until the limit, then compact) pays only after the wall, and the trigger moves the boundary
turn but not the steady-state bill.
*Evidence today:* C62 (2,780 rows, 18 compactions, arm means +134.6…+747.2, per-turn −927…+5,473,

≈830 requests, plus the ~90-request resting-window arm that removes the flagship's §6a confound.
*Risk:* H is near-certain to win directionally; the paper rides on magnitude. S and F already fail to
separate at 64k. *Venue:* MLSys / short systems track or an efficiency workshop.
*First step:* extend `cells/G3-LIMIT-V2/` (row fields `keep_last`, `keep_last_mode`,
`trigger_reason` already exist).

**P2. "Zero hits: what `cached_tokens` can and cannot tell you."**

0.27.1 `serving.py:95-96`), status-name mismatch (`status` vs `http_status`), observed miss (A1's
0/128 rows at `usage_has_details: true`), and coercion of absent to zero (49/97 U2 and 23/438 U4
records, `cells/CX-V2/null_coerce.py`) — and a hit rate is unmeasurable without the flag.
*Evidence today:* R2-VLLM 418 rows; OH1 62/64 vs A1 0/64; A1 27/27 probe hits; WIN-VLLM V2 same
model/block hit 0.868144; 0/5 core row files retain `usage`/`prompt_tokens_details`; only R2-VLLM
carries `usage_has_details`.
*Missing:* an offline diff of the A1 vs V2 launch records and grid probes (0 requests), then one A1
re-launch at `--gpu-memory-utilization 0.34` (~150 requests) with a short-prompt repeat pair and a

C 38 / H 29 / R 66) must be analysed only after `POLL-VLLM` settles; it is identity-less.
*Risk:* attribution may be mundane eviction at 0.15 gpu-mem; the methods point survives, the systems
claim shrinks. *Venue:* SC/MLSys measurement workshop or short paper.
