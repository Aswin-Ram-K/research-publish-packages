MEMORY M-1 -- Retry policy for scheduled jobs

A nightly scheduled job that exits non-zero is retried a maximum of 5 attempts, then marked dead.

Callers that want stripping must strip the parts themselves; doing it inside would make positional alignment depend on content. * The backoff counter is reset only by a manual clear. If many builds fail at once, check mirror health before bisecting individual pipelines. Do not call it on a stream wider than available RAM. That is a documentation defect, not a security defect, but it cost the most wall-clock time of anything in the exercise. Producers are never dropped, only slowed, because dropping at the queue boundary loses the ordering guarantee that the rest of the system depends on. Pushing from a build host fails authorisation and is deliberate.
