# Commercial use requires a paid licence

> **Commercial licensing is available on request.** See "How to obtain a commercial licence" below.

This project is released under the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0** (see `LICENSE`).

**Any commercial use requires a paid commercial licence.** The Noncommercial licence does not
cover commercial use, and no commercial right is granted by it. The licence has no sentence that
says "commercial use is prohibited": this follows from the scope of the grant it does make — a
copyright licence "to do everything you might do with the software that would otherwise infringe
the licensor's copyright in it **for any permitted purpose**" — read with its `No Other Rights`
section ("These terms do not imply any other licenses."). That is an **inference from those quoted
clauses**, not a quoted clause naming commercial use.

## What this includes

- Using the software, in whole or in part, for any commercial purpose.
- Using the software in or for a product, service, or internal business process.
- **Evaluating the software for a commercial purpose.** Deciding whether to buy the software for
  a commercial purpose is itself a use with an anticipated commercial application, and is
  therefore **not** covered by the Noncommercial licence. There is no free commercial evaluation
  and no trial grant — a commercial licence must be obtained first. **Attribution:** the licence
  does not use the word *"evaluation"* and grants no trial. This is an **inference from the
  licence's own qualifier**, *"without any anticipated commercial application"*, read with its
  exhaustive list of permitted purposes — a business evaluating in order to buy falls outside all
  of them. It is **not** quoted licence text.
- Use by or on behalf of a company in circumstances that are not a noncommercial purpose under
  the licence.

## What is free

Nothing here takes away the rights the Noncommercial licence grants for permitted purposes —
including personal use for research, experiment, testing, study, hobby projects and amateur
pursuits with no anticipated commercial application, and use by the noncommercial organisations
named in the licence. **Attribution:** that list is a paraphrase of the licence's `Personal Uses`
and `Noncommercial Organizations` clauses; the clauses themselves are the operative text, and
`LICENSE` carries them verbatim.

## How to obtain a commercial licence

A paid commercial licence is offered directly by the author, as a **separate written agreement**,
priced per use and arranged by contact.

**Contact:** <https://github.com/Aswin-Ram-K/research-publish-packages> — open an issue on the
published repository (its issue tracker). That is the project's own public channel, so a commercial
licence can be obtained without publishing any personal address. Nothing is granted by opening an
issue: the licence is a **separate written agreement** agreed afterwards.
