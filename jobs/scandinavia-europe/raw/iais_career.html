    Shape your future with us!

    Our mission: to bring cutting-edge research into application. Fraunhofer IAIS is one of the leading institutions in the fields of "Big Data" and "Artificial Intelligence". We build the bridge between research and industry with more than 380 smart minds from various disciplines.

    Your professional development is important to us, because we can only grow together. We value a harmonious combination of family and career as well as diversity. More good reasons to work at Fraunhofer IAIS

    Leading the way is only possible if you move forward. Fraunhofer is the world’s leading applied research organization. Here you will work together with around 30,000 colleagues to shape the future of all of us – and of your career as well.

    With us, the code is clear when it comes to AI research.

    Digitalization holds many opportunities for our society and economy – and we are developing high-tech solutions for the world of tomorrow. The fact that we also keep an eye on the risks in the process will also be part of your career code in our AI research.
