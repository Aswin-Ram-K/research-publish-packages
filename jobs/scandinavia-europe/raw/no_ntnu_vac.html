Vacancies at NTNU

At NTNU, we work for something bigger than ourselves. Our mission is clear: To create knowledge for a better world. When you join NTNU, you contribute to research, education, and innovation that make a real difference – locally, nationally, and globally.

We welcome people with diverse skills and backgrounds. At NTNU, you’ll find exciting positions in research and teaching, as well as technical and administrative roles that keep everything running. Whatever your job, you help build solutions for the future, from groundbreaking technology to sustainable societies.

You’ll be part of a university that is constantly evolving. We focus on digitalization, green transition, and international collaboration. Our strategy is about driving change and taking responsibility for the future.

Together with strong academic communities and modern infrastructure, we give you the space to grow and succeed.
