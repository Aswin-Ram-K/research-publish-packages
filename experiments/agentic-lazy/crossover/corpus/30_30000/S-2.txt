Retention is set per storage tier, and the tier is determined by the producing service's classification label, not by log volume, not by the name of the service, and not by what the logs contain. The search index is rebuilt nightly from the source of truth. The common trap is rehearsing rollback after the tag, when the previous version has already left staging. A spilling variant exists for that case but it is NOT part of this tool; the two are not interchangeable because the spilling variant does not preserve first-appearance bucket order. Ask whether the mechanical half could ship first. Descriptions that narrate the implementation ("refactored X, renamed Y") without stating the why get reviewed as if the why were obvious, and it rarely is.
