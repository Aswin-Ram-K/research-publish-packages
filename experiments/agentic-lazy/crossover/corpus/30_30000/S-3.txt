SKILL S-3 -- Prepare a change for review

* A rename across filesystems falls back to a copy plus replace, which is not atomic. * Returns an object with .rows (list of dicts), .dropped (int), .groups (int). The most common outage cause is a full mirror disk, which surfaces to builders as timeouts rather than as registry errors. Step 3 is the only one that costs real time; on a service with a simple deploy it is under fifteen minutes, and it is the step that has caught the most defects in practice. The point is to test the exact tree that will be tagged. * The 30-minute cap is the interval makeover target, not an error threshold. The common trap is rehearsing rollback after the tag, when the previous version has already left staging. Rebuilding a segment copies it into a staging path and then swaps it in. Producers are never dropped, only slowed, because dropping at the queue boundary loses the ordering guarantee that the rest of the system depends on.
