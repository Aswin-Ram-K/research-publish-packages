`est_tokens(text, bytes_per_token) = ceil(len(text.encode("utf-8")) / bpt)` (`:106-107`), so all
three growth comparisons are **byte-based**, not model-reported.

**Finding (fix is a field, not a test).** Compaction has no row-level marker of its own. The only
trace is a *separate* row written at `:2027` with `kind == "digest"`, joinable to its triggering
turn on `turn`. A turn row cannot say "I compacted here", so a reader who filters turn rows alone
will always see `digest == 0` even when compaction ran. `evicted` and `lease` are real turn-row
fields; `digest` is a row-class.

**Finding (the wall is not the cause for arm 3).** `self.walls = list(fixture.get("lease_reopen_turns") or [])`
(`:1004`). Reopen is a **declared turn list**, not a token predicate — `wall_tokens` is irrelevant to
it. All **12** runtime fixtures (`tasks-v2`…`v6`) carry `wall_tokens=65536` and **omit
`lease_reopen_turns` entirely**, so `self.walls == []` and `:1113` is false forever. That is arm 3's
reachability defect, and lowering the wall cannot fix it.

## 2. The reachable envelope, from the rows

Four controlled grids (`measure/rows-longrun-2026-10-26.jsonl` = stage1,
`…-c-2026-10-26.jsonl` = G4-RUN-C-V2, `…-v3-…`, `…-v4-…`); 576 turn rows, 0 live markers.

**Observed max prompt in the four grids: `34,279` tokens** — `rows-longrun-c-2026-10-26.jsonl`,
**turn 48**, arm `transcript-grow`, run `longrun-c-v2-2026-10-26-transcript-grow-r1`.

ARM-INTEGRITY's `34279` is grid-scoped; the 1.9× wall ratio is too (65536/51216 = **1.28×** corpus-wide).

Arithmetic, for `prompt(t) = p1 + slope·(t−1)`, firing when `prompt(t) > wall`:

* **G4 grow** — `t = 1 + (65536 − 2114)/684.36 = 93.7 → T94`. At 48 turns it needs
  **`unreachable at 48 turns` (needs T94)**.
* **G4 compact** — `t = 1 + (65536 − 2114)/75.02 = 846.4 → T847` → **`unreachable at 48 turns` (needs T847)**.
* **stage1 grow** T1654, **stage1 compact** T571, **v3 grow** T1046, **v4 grow** T1010 — all
  **`unreachable at 48 turns`** at the frozen wall.
* **lease-reopen** — `unreachable at ANY N` (key absent), not a turn-count problem.

Inverting, the wall that fires each arm **at T24**, leaving 24 turns of post-wall data:

* G4 grow: `2114 + 684.36×23 = 17,854` → **`wall ≤ 17,853`**
* G4 compact: `2114 + 75.02×23 = 3,839` → **`wall ≤ 3,838`**
* stage1 grow 2,755 · stage1 compact 4,440 · v3 grow 3,548 · v4 grow 5,422.

Anti-degeneracy floor: the wall must exceed the T1 working-set prompt (stage1 1870, G4 2114,
v3 2154, v4 4020) or the arm evicts/digests on every turn. **Every growth arm has a legal wall
that fires within 48 turns**, so the `unreachable at 48 turns` verdicts are properties of the
*frozen parameters*, not of the arms.

**Caveat that must not be buried.** Inside G4, grow's answers averaged ~684 tok/turn while
compact's averaged ~75. Both arms replay history, so the slope gap is partly **answer verbosity,
not treatment**. A wall calibrated off one cell's slope will not fire at the same turn in another
rep of the same arm. Calibrate per cell from a dry-run, never copy a wall across reps.

## 4. Parameters that make all three fire at 48 turns

```json
{
  "N": 48,
  "wall_tokens": 3838,
  "lease_reopen_turns": [23, 40],
  "bytes_per_token": 4.0914,
  "expected_digests": 2
}
```

| key | kind | why this value |
|---|---|---|
| `wall_tokens` | **fixture** field (versioned) | 3838 = G4-compact's T24 crossing; fires compact at T24 and grow from ~T4, both above the 2114 anti-degeneracy floor |
| `lease_reopen_turns` | **fixture** field (versioned) | `[23,40]` — the documented `[23,56]` has its second wall past a 48-turn cell |
| `N` | **fixture** field, frozen by PARM-1 | stays 48 |
| `bytes_per_token` | **fixture** field | unchanged from `LR-01.v5.json` |
| `expected_digests` | **runner** constant | `run_longrun.py:1133-1135` returns `2` unconditionally; if the wall fires 3+ times the cell hits `BUDGET ABORT` (exit 4) because `budget = n_turns + 2` (`:424`). Making it fixture-derived is a runner change, not a parameter. |

At `wall=3838` the compact cell digests at ~T24 and again near T48 after the reset (`self.raw = self.raw[-4:]`), i.e. **exactly 2** — inside the hardcoded budget by zero margin. This is a knife-edge, and it is why `expected_digests` is the first thing a follow-up card should fixture-ise.

## 5. Price of a grid that fires

* A\* today: `4 arms × 48 turns × 3 reps = 576` turn requests × `1.374532` = **792** ([BUDGET-SCHEDULE.md](experiments/ptr-engine/measure/BUDGET-SCHEDULE.md):43).
* A\* that fires at 48 turns: **798** — `+2 digests × 3 compact cells = +6`, **+0.76 %**. Eviction and reopen are host-side and cost **zero** requests.
* If the *registered* two-wall design must fire as registered (digest #2 at T53, reopen #2 at T56), `N ≥ 56`: `4 × 56 × 3 = 672` × factor = **924**, plus 6 digests = **930**, **+138 (+17.4 %)**.
* Ledger remaining 3,219: **four** firing grids fit at 48 turns (3219 ÷ 798 = 4), **three** at N=56 (3219 ÷ 930 = 3).

**Verdict on the horizon: keep 48.** All three markers can be non-zero at N=48 with the block
above, for +6 requests. 48 → 56 is required only if the claim is "the pre-registered *second*
wall fired", which is a stronger claim than "the treatment fired" and costs a whole grid.
