## The asymmetry (reproduced before the fix)
* `resextract serve` (`cli._resolve_serve` L428-433): non-loopback + `auto` ⇒ ValueError, exit 2;
  loopback `auto` ⇒ `secrets.token_urlsafe(32)` **printed** (L490-491).
* `python -m resextract.dashboard` L57 `if not is_loopback(bound) and not args.token:` — literal
  `auto` is truthy, so it passed and became `create_app(token='auto')` (L78). No `auto` keyword.
* Live: `--token auto --host 192.168.2.187 --allow-remote --port 8971` ⇒ **Uvicorn running**,
  anon `/` **401**, `x-dashboard-token: auto` **200**. Belief: "auto is safe". Reality: a LAN
  listener whose whole secret is four characters.

## Rule (one sentence)
`--token auto` never means the literal secret `auto`: on loopback it generates a strong random
token and prints it; on a non-loopback bind it is refused (exit 2, nothing bound) because the token
there gates every route and must be operator-chosen.

## Change
`__main__.py`: new `_resolve_token()`, non-loopback refusal for `('', 'auto')` **before**
`create_app`/`uvicorn.run`, `resextract dashboard: control token: <token>` printed when generated,
non-fatal warning for <16-char explicit tokens on loopback. Explicit literal tokens still work.
`check_bind_host` remains the single bind decision; `allow_remote` is never derived from `--host`;
the all-routes remote-read gate in `app.py` is untouched.
