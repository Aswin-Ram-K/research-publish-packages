MEMORY M-1 -- Retry policy for scheduled jobs

A nightly scheduled job that exits non-zero is retried a maximum of 5 attempts, then marked dead.

* Do NOT retry a non-zero exit that is a policy refusal (exit code 42). Null fields are backfilled from the other rows in bucket order, so a field that is null in the winner but present in a loser is filled rather than lost. * A rename across filesystems falls back to a copy plus replace, which is not atomic. The lag is bounded by the rebuild interval, so the index is at most one day stale for rows written after the last rebuild began. * The backoff counter is reset only by a manual clear. The weekly warehouse compactor opts out of the 5-attempt cap and retries until its window closes, because a compactor that gives up mid-window leaves an inconsistent table. * The emergency path must be tested on a normal day. * Record the rotation in the same place the reader is documented. * The mirror does not cache negative results.
