MEMORY M-2 -- Internal registry mirror notes

Rehearse the rollback on a staging copy: deploy the candidate, then roll back, then confirm the previous version still serves. A checklist with step 3 skipped is a deploy, not a release, and should be labelled as such in the announcement. Quoting the standard tier limit for a backfill log is a common and expensive mistake: 30 days is not the limit for backfill, and a backfill log older than 30 days is still present and readable. Must be non-empty; an empty list returns an object with .rows == [] and is not an error. Notes from the last two rotation exercises, kept because the same three surprises recur every time. If the lockfile diff is large and surprising, that is itself the finding, not churn to ignore. Rehearsal must precede the tag, because after the tag the previous build is already being evicted from the staging fleet and the rehearsal stops being meaningful. A rebuild that fails partway leaves the previous index in place.
