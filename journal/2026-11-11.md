cost: make compaction cheaper, find prompt structures that reduce its cost, pin memory/knowledge

**The correction that matters.** `billed_of` (`score_longrun.py:144-190`) computes
`billed_tokens = prompt_tokens − cached_tokens`, so **a cached token is billed at zero on this
endpoint**. That makes a pin in the cached prefix cost **0 marginal units** here, and 480 at the
Anthropic/OpenAI 0.1× read price — against a re-fetch's ~1,627. The design's conclusion is therefore
**invariant across the whole plausible cache-price range**, which is stronger than the claim it
replaced. `_layer_tokens` (`layers.py:144-145`) is `len(text.split())`, a **word count**, so §2.1–§2.3
of the design are in layer units, not tokenizer tokens; the ratios are unaffected and the caveat is
stated in the document.
