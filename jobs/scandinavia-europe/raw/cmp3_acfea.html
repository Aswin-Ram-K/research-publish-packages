Vacancies

Interested in working @ CWI? If we have vacancies, you can check them on this page.

CWI is a an excellent employer offering a pleasant working environment, and cares for its employees. We greatly value diversity and inclusion, offering room for personal development, good terms of employment, peer contacts and events.

To apply for a position, go to the vacancy and click the “apply” button. You can then enter your application and information in our application portal. This system ensures your privacy and the security of your data. You can always edit or delete your data through the portal.

CWI follows the NVP Recruitment Code, that provides a standard for transparent and fair recruitment and selection procedures.
