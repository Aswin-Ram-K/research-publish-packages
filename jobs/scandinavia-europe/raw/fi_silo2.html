    We Push the Frontier of AI on AMD Compute Platforms

    AMD Silo AI is a leading AI lab that helps customers develop and deploy advanced AI models and solutions optimized for leadership compute platforms.

    As a global AI center of excellence with over 300 AI scientists and 125+ PhDs, we combine deep scientific knowledge with practical technology understanding to help organizations integrate, deploy and scale AI effectively. Our work spans from groundbreaking research to enterprise-ready AI solutions.

    R&D for Frontier AI

    We advance the boundaries of what's possible while solving the most challenging real-world problems, combining world-class AI research with deep industry expertise.

    Enterprise AI Solutions

    AI creates value by elevating digital products and services, enhancing their capability and impact. Together with our compute leadership, we empower businesses to integrate AI effectively, transforming technology potential into real-world results.

    AI for People

    We build human-centric and trustworthy AI that frees human minds for meaningful work and empowers creativity, ensuring technology continues to serve society while augmenting human capabilities.

    AMD Silo AI Compute to Impact Initiative

    AMD Silo AI has been awarded funding from Business Finland to lead Compute to Impact, a five-year initiative coordinating over €100 million in AI research and development investments. The program is designed to strengthen Finland’s position as a global leader in applied AI by accelerating innovation, talent development, and ecosystem-wide collaboration.

    The Next Generation of Industry-Leading Products and Services is Driven by AI

    Enabling you to go from solid hardware foundations to deploying AI at scale,  we build AI to drive your competitive edge.

    Enable AI

    Enable value creation with AI by building a solid base of competences and technology combined with the right hardware to manage AI compute workloads, generating a flow of opportunities.

    Optimize AI

    Optimize models and workloads to efficiently run on select hardware with optimal performance, while making sure the right data, tech, and competences are in place.

    Deploy AI

    From scattered AI projects to AI at the core and in production, deploy AI as part of larger systems and operations with efficiency, predictable progress, and ability to scale.

    From Inception, to Optimization, and Production.

    Rethink your approach to AI. Deliver AI technologies that truly benefit your customers and end-users. We help you on every step of the journey, from early product planning to running AI efficiently in production, scaling your competitive edge with AI.

    World-Class AI Lab to Elevate Your AI Capabilities

    Deliver scalable AI features quickly and with confidence through end-to-end implementation across your technology stack. With engineering experience in a myriad of technologies and data platforms, solving challenges across various use cases, our team of 300 AI scientists including 125+ PhDs have delivered more than 200 production-grade AI implementations.

    AI Platforms & Solutions

    The SiloGen™ platform unites the best of AMD software, reliable hardware & infrastructure, and AI expertise in a one-stop AI development and deployment solution. Engineered for enterprise AI, the SiloGen platform is tailored to meet the needs of modern enterprises.

    Enterprise AI Reference Stack

    By connecting key open-source AI frameworks and Gen AI models with an enterprise-ready Kubernetes platform, enterprises can go from bare metal compute to production-grade AI in minutes, minimizing the time from AI experimentation to large scale production on AMD compute platforms.
