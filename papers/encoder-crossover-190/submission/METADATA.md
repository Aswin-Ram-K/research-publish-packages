## Category justification

- **Primary cs.CL** — the work is an NLP text-classification benchmark comparison: encoders vs a
  4B decoder LLM on a 190-decision classification fixture, with a literature claim about the
  "encoder wins" result. cs.CL is where every cited work lives (ModernBERT, GLiNER, GLiClass,
  Seq vs Seq, the LLM-text-classification reviews).
- **Cross-list cs.LG** — the contribution is a controlled statistical comparison (paired arms,
  power/`psi` calculation, majority-class and shuffled-label controls, human-Gold vacuity), and
  the method lesson is about evaluation design rather than language structure.
- **Optional cs.AI** — only if the author wants the decision-model/harness angle seen by the AI
  systems audience; one cross-list is enough, two cost nothing but dilute.

## Licence note

CC BY 4.0 is **not** the arXiv default. The default is the "arXiv.org perpetual, non-exclusive
license to distribute". Choosing CC BY 4.0 is allowed (it is one of the offered options) and is
**irrevocable once the paper is announced**. Practically: arXiv must then comply with CC BY 4.0's
attribution and termination/revival terms, the first page carries a licence stamp, and anyone may
reuse the paper with attribution. Refs: <https://info.arxiv.org/help/license/index.html>.
