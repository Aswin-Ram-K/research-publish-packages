  1. Open

    Simons Collaborations in Mathematics and the Physical Sciences - 2028 Request for Applications

    Funding Opportunity

    The aim of the Simons Collaborations in MPS program is to stimulate progress on fundamental scientific questions of major importance in mathematics, theoretical physics and theoretical computer science.

    Apply by October 29, 2026

  2. Open

    Simons Historically Black Colleges and Universities Faculty Fellowship - 2027 Request for Applications

    Funding Opportunity

    Supports junior HBCU faculty with protected research time to advance their work, pursue grants and publications, build collaborations, and create pathways for students into graduate STEM programs.

    Apply by November 12, 2026

  3. Open

    Simons Centers of Excellence at Historically Black Colleges and Universities - 2027 Request for Applications

    Funding Opportunity

    Supports up to two HBCU Centers of Excellence in mathematics, physics and ecology and evolutionary biology to advance research, collaboration and pathways to graduate STEM programs.

    Apply by November 12, 2026

  4. Open

    Utilizing the Full Power of Empire AI - 2027 Request for Applications

    Funding Opportunity

    The Simons Foundation (SF) intends to support cutting-edge mathematics and basic science research, drawing on the advanced computational power of the Empire AI. Toward this goal, SF plans to initiate a new program to fund six to 12 proposals through a competitive open call to the Empire AI research community.

    Apply by December 1, 2026

  5. Open

    Simons Postdoctoral Fellowships in Plant Biology – 2027 Request for Applications

    Funding Opportunity

    These postdoctoral fellowships support candidates who intend to pursue a career in basic research on fundamental problems in plant biology. This program aims to support researchers seeking to understand the inner workings of plants at the level of the whole organism or below.

    Apply by December 3, 2026

  6. Open

    SPARK Research Match Expansion — 2026-2027 Request for Applications

    Funding Opportunity

    The SPARK Research Match Expansion request for applications (RFA) encourages and supports applications for SPARK Research Match studies from principal investigators to increase SPARK research opportunities to individuals whose genetic ancestries have been undersampled in clinical research.

    Apply by December 31, 2026

  7. Open

    Fellows-to-Faculty - 2027 Request for Applications

    Funding Opportunity

    The Fellows-to-Faculty Award program supports talented early career scientists by facilitating their transition into tenure-track faculty positions. The focus of this program is the $600,000 USD three (3) year faculty research grant.

    Apply by January 13, 2027

  8. Open

    Scientific Software Research Faculty Award - 2027–2028 Request for Applications

    Funding Opportunity

    The MPS division invites applications for the Scientific Software Research Faculty Award, which supports new research professor positions in existing academic departments to be filled by scientific software-focused researchers no later than September 1, 2027.

    Apply by January 21, 2027

  9. Open

    Travel Support for Mathematicians - 2027 Request for Applications

    Funding Opportunity

    The MPS division invites applications for its Travel Support for Mathematicians program, which is intended to stimulate collaboration in the field primarily through the funding of travel and related expenditures.

    Apply by January 27, 2027

  10. Open

    New Ideas - 2026 Request for Applications

    Funding Opportunity

    The goal of the New Ideas Award is to provide early stage support for bold, exploratory research that tests novel hypotheses and opens new conceptual or experimental directions in the study of autism and related neurodevelopmental disorders (NDD).

  11. Open

    Targeted Grants in MPS

    Funding Opportunity

    The MPS division invites rolling letters of intent for the Targeted Grants in MPS program, which supports high-risk theoretical mathematics, physics and computer science projects of exceptional promise and scientific importance on a case-by-case basis.
