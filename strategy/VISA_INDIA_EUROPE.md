# India & Europe/Scandinavia routes for an independent AI/agent-runtime researcher

**Unit:** UH2 · **Date compiled:** 2026-10-15 · **All URLs accessed:** 2026-10-15
**Profile this maps:** researcher in AI/agent-runtime systems with an independent empirical record
(agent state kernel "Consonance": ~39 research records, a working paper, a findings register, an
external-evidence review, an open-source codebase under AGPL-3.0). School has stopped issuing
OPT/CPT ⇒ **US student-status-dependent routes are out**. Wanted: routes in **India** and
**Europe / preferably Scandinavia** that are **independent of student status**.

> ## ⚠️ Not legal advice
> I am not a lawyer, and this document is not legal advice. It is a source-traced inventory of
> published government and funder rules, compiled from the official pages cited below. Immigration
> rules change (several of the Swedish and Danish rules cited here changed in 2026), and the
> application of a rule to a specific person depends on facts not known here — including
> **nationality**, which this document explicitly treats as an open branch. Before acting, confirm
> every load-bearing point with the authority itself or a licensed immigration adviser in the
> destination country.

## Method, and what "verified" means here

- Only **official government and funder sources** are used for load-bearing claims: Migrationsverket
  (SE), Ny i Danmark / SIRI (DK), UDI-linked material via the EURAXESS Norway national portal (NO),
  EURAXESS Finland (FI), IND (NL), `gesetze-im-internet.de` (DE federal law), European Commission
  (MSCA), ERC, Research Council of Norway, ANRF/Startup India/IndiaAI (IN).
- Every verified row carries **URL + page date + a short verbatim excerpt**.
- **`local_web_search` returned no results for every query** and the harness `web_search` tool had no
  API key, so no search backend was available. Several authority sites hard-blocked every fetch path:
  `udi.no` (Azure WAF, HTTP 403 on curl, the crawler, scraper, and an extraction proxy), `migri.fi`
  (Cloudflare, HTTP 403 on all paths), and `archive.org` returned HTTP 429 for the whole session. Where
  that happened, the claim is **not** stated as fact — it goes to the `UNVERIFIED` list with the
  blocking reason.
- Where an authority site was unreachable, the **official EU national portal (EURAXESS)** for that
  country is used and **labelled as such**; EURAXESS national portals are run by national research
  bodies, not by the immigration authority, so they are load-bearing only for what they themselves
  state.

**The honest headline on funding:** every well-documented scheme in this table that funds an
*independent* researcher either (a) requires a PhD (MSCA PF, ERC StG, most national postdoc
programmes), or (b) requires a host institution to apply through (FRIPRO, NordForsk, Novel
Nordisk/Carlsberg/Villum, Research Council of Finland). None of the pages verified here offers money
to an unaffiliated individual. **The practical consequence: the funding route is downstream of
getting a host, not a substitute for one.** The single most useful verified fact for this profile is
MSCA PF's requirement that "the beneficiary … needs to recruit the researcher" — i.e. a fellowship,
once won, *is* the employment relationship that a work/researcher permit is built on.

## 3. India

India is the route that needs no immigration permission at all for an Indian citizen — but the
**nationality branch is unknown here** and it changes everything, so it is stated explicitly:

> **Nationality branch (UNRESOLVED).** This unit was not told whether the researcher holds Indian
> citizenship, OCI, PIO or another status. If they are an **Indian citizen**, they need **no visa or
> permit to work in India** and the entire section below is an employment/funding question, not an

## 6. Ranked recommendation — the 3 most realistic routes, with a concrete first step

Ranking is by *distance from this profile to a live permit*, and it deliberately weights
**student-status independence** and **host acquisition** above prestige.

### 🥇 1. Denmark — Pay Limit Scheme (DKK 552,000 job offer)
**Why first.** It is the only route verified here that has *no educational requirement at all*
("It is **not** required that you have a specific educational background or that your job is within a
specific professional field"), so it does not put the researcher's record on trial against a
credential test; processing is **1 month**; and it is a residence **and work** permit that leads
onward. The barrier is purely commercial: a Danish employer willing to pay the threshold.
**First step (concrete).** Target Danish employers that hire agent-runtime/infrastructure engineers
and can pay ≥ DKK 552,000 — including SIRI-**certified** companies, which additionally unlocks the
**Fast-track scheme** ([nyidanmark Fast track](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply/Work/Fast-track)).
Put the AGPL-3.0 codebase and the working paper in front of them as the credential. Verify the
current pay-limit figure on [the Pay Limit page](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply/Work/Pay-Limit-Scheme)
immediately before any offer is negotiated.

### 🥈 2. Sweden — EU Blue Card, or a researcher permit hosted by a **company**
**Why second.** Two independent verified doors that do not require a doctorate: (a) the **EU Blue
Card**, where "five years of relevant professional experience" can replace the 180-credit degree
requirement, with a threshold of **SEK 53,625/month**; and (b) the **researcher permit**, where a
**business** can be the approved research principal and the requirement is a doctorate **or**
eligibility to start a doctoral programme, with a maintenance level of only **SEK 9,700/month** —
much easier to meet than the Blue Card threshold. Sweden also has the **best verified PR story**:
three years of continuous research/Blue Card/doctoral permits now gives permanent residence.
If no employer will pay SEK 53,625, the **ordinary work permit at SEK 34,470/month** is the fallback.
**First step (concrete).** Approach a Swedish **research institute or a company with a research
function** — not a university only — and ask specifically whether it is an approved research
principal that will sign a **hosting agreement (*mottagningsavtal*)** ([hosting-agreement form
231011](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/work/employee-or-self-employed/researchers.html)).
In parallel, ask VR-approved research principals and RISE-type institutes. Confirm the research
principal's approval status with the Swedish Research Council (VR), which the Migration Agency names
as the approving body.

### 🥉 3. Germany — EU Blue Card without a degree on the ICT sub-route (§18g(2))
**Why third** (and why it is a genuine route, not a consolation): Germany has a **statutory** ICT
Blue Card that waives the academic-qualification requirement for ISCO-08 groups 133/25 with ≥3 years'
experience and a salary at 45.3% of the pension contribution ceiling. That is the cleanest legal
answer in Europe to "AI/software person without a PhD". It ranks third only because the researcher
has no established German network and Because the pay threshold, while lower than Sweden's 50% band,
still requires an employer.
**First step (concrete).** Target German employers hiring for ICT roles that fall in ISCO-08 group 25
(ICT professionals), and put the §18g(2) route in the offer negotiation explicitly; then confirm the
current published minimum salary for the year (the statute requires the Federal Ministry of the
Interior to publish the minimum salaries **in the Bundesanzeiger** by 31 December of the preceding
year — §18g(7)). Use the [§18b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__18b.html) /
[§18g](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__18g.html) texts as the primary source with
any employer or adviser.

**Funding route, stated honestly:** MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships and ERC Starting Grants are the
best-documented independent-researcher funding instruments in Europe, and both verified pages
**require a PhD**. They are therefore **not** near-term routes for this profile unless a doctorate
exists. The realistic funding path is to get a host first and then apply for scheme money through
that host — FRIPRO ("Approved Norwegian research organisations can apply") and NordForsk make this
explicit.

**India, stated honestly:** for an **Indian citizen** the entire question collapses into employment
and grant-hunting, with **no immigration permission required**. The ANRF scheme family (Ramanujan
Fellowship, N-PDF, Prime Minister Professorship, PAIR and the rest) is the correct target set for a
returning researcher, but **no eligibility, amount or deadline could be verified in this run**, so
nothing about amounts should be assumed. The **first step** is to open the Ramanujan Fellowship and
N-PDF pages on [anrfonline.in](https://anrfonline.in/ANRF/ramanujan-fellowship) directly in a browser
(they are JS-rendered and were not readable by this unit's fetchers) and read the eligibility text
off the live page — that is a 10-minute task that removes the largest single `UNVERIFIED` block in
this document.
