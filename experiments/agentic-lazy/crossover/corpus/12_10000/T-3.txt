TOOL T-3 -- write_note_json

* Retry on an exponential schedule starting at 60 seconds and doubling each attempt, capped at 30 minutes between attempts. An artifact nobody has pulled in 90 days is evicted and re-fetched from upstream on the next request. Reproduce locally from a clean checkout at the failing commit. Longer documents therefore hurt rebuild time before they hurt query latency, and the relationship is worse than linear because longer documents also produce more overlapping chunks. Emitting trailing empties is deliberate: positional alignment beats tidiness, and a caller indexing into a record wants positions to mean the same thing in every row. A reader therefore sees either the old file or the new file and never a partial one. The backfill tier is deliberately not in the table above.
