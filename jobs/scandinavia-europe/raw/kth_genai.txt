Current genAI models, used for generating music, images, text, and other media, rely on vast data sets and require significant computational resources. Training genAI models and employing them on a global scale contributes to substantial energy consumption, resource depletion, and increased carbon emissions, exacerbating environmental harm. Our project aims to develop and promote an innovative, sustainable alternative to existing large and commercial genAI systems. It aims to reduce the environmental footprint of genAI technologies while fostering positive social impacts by prioritizing small, ethically sourced data sets, local models and compute, and encouraging sustainable behavior among users.</p><p>The research and development conducted in the project can be regarded as situated within the area of human-centered AI. FrugalAI has the following objectives:</p><ol><li>Estimate the environmental impact of current big-data genAI.</li><li>Construct a set of open-source frugal genAI tools based on small, ethical data.</li><li>Map the impact on social sustainability that emerges from using frugal genAI.</li><li>Compare the environmental impact of these tools with big-data genAI tools.</li><li>Propose and apply evaluation metrics that consider resource use.</li><li>Investigate implications for other genAI applications (e.g., image generation).</li></ol><p>The postdoctoral researcher, having a background related to HCI and machine learning, will contribute to all objectives over a period of two years at 100% time.
