Employee Resource Groups

Our Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) are inclusive and welcoming to all. They’re just one of the many initiatives at SRI designed to help foster a sense of community and belonging throughout the institute.

A network of Black professionals and allies, Black@SRI fosters an inclusive, supportive workplace environment through empowerment, mentorship, collaboration, and celebration of Black employees’ diverse experiences and contributions. Black@SRI helps create a sense of belonging at SRI, promote professional growth, and support opportunities for networking and allyship. 

The mission of the Parents & Caregivers of Children (PaCC) is to provide SRI parents and caregivers of children with confidence, resources, and knowledge to help balance their work and family. Through education, collaboration, and advocacy, PaCC strives to make working parents and caregivers of children feel supported.

To make SRI a welcoming workplace for current and future team members from the LGBTQ+ community, Prism seeks to create a fair, safe community while raising awareness of the value of full inclusion and authenticity. We provide an open social and networking forum for LGBTQ+ team members and allies, and we give back to our local communities to empower a broader culture of belonging.

The Rising & Early Career Professionals Employee Resource Group (RISE) aims to be a dynamic collective of emerging talent and early career individuals that fosters an environment of growth and empowerment. We aspire to thrive in our professional journeys while supporting one another in navigating the complexities of the workplace.

The Women in Business (WIB) employee resource group was created to provide a peer support and mentorship program, improve the pipeline of gender diverse talent through outreach, and promote opportunities for women across the company.

The Women in STEM Employee Resource Group (WISE) was created to support emerging female talent at SRI and provide opportunities for women employees to advocate for one another and foster a positive professional culture. WISE sponsors events that promote SRI’s value of belonging and creates a forum to share knowledge and encourage mentorship.

Internships

SRI enriches the science and technology community by training and mentoring undergraduate and graduate student interns. Our internship participants experience a broad spectrum of activities from learning how to write grant proposals and client reports to articulating ideas and conducting hands-on research.

Student interns have opportunities to present their work, attend seminars, and engage with their peers. Students benefit from having an assigned advisor and from working in an environment that includes some of the R&D community’s most innovative contributors.

All interns must have current full-time undergraduate or graduate student status at an accredited college institution, plus unrestricted authorization to work in the United States. Positions are designed to supplement education and career direction. Job duties are tied to a specific project and training is provided in parallel to the classroom curriculum.

Postdoctoral fellowships

SRI enriches the science and technology community by shaping postdoctoral students into professional researchers. Postdoctoral fellowship positions are available to U.S. and non-U.S. citizens of high scientific and scholastic achievement who have recently received their doctoral degrees and want to continue their research and training under the guidance of a senior SRI staff member.

Postdoctoral positions are usually for a one-year period and may be extended by mutual agreement in one-year increments for a second or third year (completion of the period is not obligatory for either party). In many cases, postdoctoral fellows at SRI have the opportunity to become career researchers or scientists. SRI provides full benefits to our postdoctoral fellows, and relocation assistance is provided.

Export control and employment policy

U.S. Export Laws restrict the transfer of technology to citizens of certain countries. Your employment at SRI is contingent upon SRI’s obtaining any required export licenses. If you are a foreign national from one of the following embargoed countries — Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Sudan, and Syria — SRI is unable to hire you due to U.S. Government trade and economic sanctions.

Background check

Please note that employment with SRI International is contingent upon the successful completion of a background check.

Frequently asked questions

The best way to be considered for a position at SRI is to complete an application and submit your resume through our career portal.

All job openings can be found on our career portal. You can customize your search by entering key words, job category, title, and location.

You can submit your resume and it will be in our database and available for recruiters to search.

Once you’ve created an account through our career portal, you can apply for jobs that meet your interest. Once you’ve applied, you can track the status of your application under the applications tab.

You’ll receive an acknowledgement of receipt of your application and then you should receive a status update within two to three weeks of submitting your application.

If you need assistance or an accommodation when applying for a position through our website, please contact us at employment@sri.com.

We are proud to be an Equal Opportunity Employer, making decisions without regard to race, color, religion, creed, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, marital status, national origin, age, veteran status, disability, or any other protected class. If you need assistance or an accommodation when applying for a position through our website, please contact us at employment@sri.com. Decisions regarding these requests will be handled on a case-by-case basis. For other inquiries or technical difficulties, email employment@sri.com.
