## Deliverables

- `GitObjectSource(root, commit_sha)` — resolves `git cat-file blob <commit>:<path>`,
  records the blob sha (`ls-tree`) beside the content sha256, refuses `..`,
  absolute paths and anything under `.worktrees/`, raises typed errors for
  absent/directory/symlink paths, and **never falls back to the working tree**.
  `PinnedFile.write_to` is atomic and strips setuid/setgid/sticky;
  `verify_pinned` re-hashes the destination; `commit_exists`/`resolve_head`
  are the repo probes. `WorkingTreeSource` preserves the old behaviour explicitly.
- `detect_concurrent_writer(target, window_s=5)` — one bounded window over HEAD +
  a tracked-file `(size, mtime_ns)` snapshot, verdict
  `quiescent | active_writer | head_moved`, never raises.
  `assert_quiescent` raises `TargetBusyError(signal)` unless quiescent or
  `allow_active=True`. Reuses the staleness module's git runner, tracked-file
  listing and ignore machinery (no duplicated git plumbing).
- `docs/LIVE_TARGET_NOTES.md` — the live environment fact.
