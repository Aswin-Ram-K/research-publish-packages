Want to work with us?

The University of Iceland is one of the country's largest employers. We rely on a diverse team of employees to provide quality education and conduct impactful academic research. We aim to foster a vibrant community, enhancing our position as a leading educational institution.

Surveys indicate that UI employees enjoy high levels of job satisfaction, good morale and opportunities for advancement.

Benefits include professional development, social events, sabbaticals and health services such as annual flu vaccinations and UI wellbeing hub.

Research management (Grant Writer specialist)

A full-time position for a Grant writer specialist is open for application at the Science Institute, School of Engineering and Natural Sciences at the University of Iceland. The role involves leading and supporting the pre-award preparation and application process for international research grants in the fields of physical sciences, natural sciences, and engineering.The specialist will work closely with researchers and international research teams on the development and presentation of competitive grant applications.

Field of work

  • Advice, development, planning, and writing of grant applications (Grant Writer), particularly for European competitive funds such as Horizon Europe.
  • Support and guidance for researchers and international research groups in shaping research ideas and clearly communicating scientific novelty.
  • Assistance with the design and writing of work packages and project plans.
  • Analysis of grant opportunities in physical sciences, natural sciences, and engineering, along with advice on aligning them with the institute's strategy and priorities.
  • Support for building international research collaborations and connections with foreign partners.
  • Organization and management of courses, workshops, and educational seminars on grant application writing.

Qualification requirements

  • Master's degree in physical sciences, natural sciences, engineering, or related fields; a PhD is an advantage.
  • Strong academic insight and the ability to quickly understand scientific concepts and subjects.
  • Critical and analytical thinking, with the ability to structure and communicate scientific topics clearly.
  • Experience in a university and research environment, along with a good understanding of research practices, terminology, and researcher needs, is an advantage.
  • Experience in preparing, writing, structuring, and reviewing large international research grants is an advantage.
  • Experience with regulations, evaluation criteria, and application processes of European grants¿including the EU Funding & Tenders Portal¿is an advantage.
  • Experience collaborating with diverse, international research teams is an advantage.
  • Experience in conducting courses, advising, and guiding specialists is an advantage.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Excellent proficiency in English, both spoken and written (academic text).
  • Proficiency in Icelandic, both spoken and written, is an advantage.
  • Organizational skills, independence, and a service-oriented mindset.

Application process

It is desirable that the successful candidate can start as soon as possible.

Salary is according to the current collective agreement between the Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs and the Association of University Teachers (Félag háskólakennara).

The following documents must accompany the application: 

  • Curriculum vitae 
  • Cover letter describing how the applicant meets the qualification requirements stated in the advertisement and what they consider they can contribute to the position 
  • Certified copies of diplomas 
  • Contact information for referees 

All applications will be answered, and applicants will be informed of the outcome once a decision has been made. Applications may remain valid for six months after the application deadline. 

In hiring for positions at the University of Iceland, the University`s equality policy is taken into account. Attention is also drawn to the University of Iceland`s language policy. 

Since 1911, the University of Iceland has been a leading institution for business and societal progress in Iceland. It is a forward-looking research university and an active participant in international scientific collaboration. 

The University of Iceland is the largest university in the country, offering nearly 400 programs at the undergraduate, master `s, and doctoral levels. Approximately 14,000 students are enrolled, including over 2,000 international students. 

More than 1,000 staff members work at the University of Iceland. The university emphasizes attracting a diverse community of employees and students to an international academic environment, where academic freedom, professionalism, equality, diversity, and inclusion are prioritized.

Postdoctoral Researcher in Genomic Instability and Cancer Development

Applications are invited for a full-time postdoctoral position investigating the role of MITF in maintaining genome stability, with a particular focus on DNA replication and DNA repair. The project is funded for three years by the Icelandic Research Fund of the Icelandic Centre for Research (Rannís) and will be conducted at the Biomedical Center within the Faculty of Medicine, University of Iceland.The project will employ a range of advanced research methods, including cancer cell culture, CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing, assessment of DNA damage and replication stress (e.g. DNA fibre assays and 53BP1 foci) and analysis of protein-DNA interactions (e.g. CUT&RUN), as well as other molecular biology, biochemistry and cell biology techniques.The project will be supervised by Stefan Sigurdsson, Professor at the Faculty of Medicine, University of Iceland.

Field of work

  • Investigating the role of MITF in DNA replication and DNA-damage repair
  • Determining how MITF contributes to the maintenance of genome stability
  • Examining the effects of MITF on replication stress and the DNA-damage response
  • Investigating the roles of different MITF isoforms in cellular function
  • Publishing and presenting research findings at scientific conferences and in peer-reviewed journals
  • Contributing to the day-to-day management of the research project

Qualification requirements

  • A PhD in biochemistry, molecular life sciences or a related discipline. The successful candidate must have completed their degree by the start of employment.
  • Research experience in a laboratory using biochemical, molecular biological and cell biological methods
  • Experience in mammalian cell culture
  • Good computer skills are desirable
  • Proficiency in both spoken and written English
  • Ability to work independently and strong interpersonal skills

The applicant must have completed the doctoral degree within the past seven years at the time the appointment begins. However, it is permitted to take into account the rules of the grant, illness, parental leave, and similar circumstances when assessing the total time elapsed since  completion of the doctoral degree, in accordance with the University of Iceland`s procedures for postdoctoral appointments.

Application process

The candidate is expected to begin in December 2026 or by further agreement.

The following documents must accompany the application:

  • Cover letter describing how the applicant meets the qualification requirements stated in the advertisement and what they consider they can contribute to the position (mas. 2 pages)
  • Curriculum vitae
  • Certified copies of diplomas
  • Contact information for two referees, including their relationship to the applicant, email address, and phone number.
  • Overview of publications.
  • Applicants are also invited to submit a PDF version of their doctoral thesis (in any language), as well as other relevant publications (maximum of 5 documents).

All applications will be answered, and applicants will be informed of the outcome once a decision has been made. Applications may remain valid for six months after the application deadline.

In hiring for positions at the University of Iceland, the University`s equality policy is taken into account. Attention is also drawn to the University of Iceland`s language policy.

More than 1,000 staff members work at the University of Iceland. The university emphasizes attracting a diverse community of employees and students to an international academic environment, where academic freedom, professionalism, equality, diversity, and inclusion are prioritized.

Assistant Professor in Geochemistry and Environmental Geology

The Faculty of Earth Sciences at the University of Iceland invites applications for a full-time position as Assistant Professor in Geochemistry and Environmental Geology. According to the University of Iceland's regulations, academic duties are divided into three main areas: research (40%), teaching (48%), and administration (12%).

Field of work

  • Teach undergraduate courses and courses at the graduate-level in the candidate's area of research specialization
  • Supervision of undergraduate and graduate students
  • Lead and contribute to research projects in geochemistry and environmental geoscience, utilizing Iceland's unique geological setting
  • Contribute to the development and organization of academic programs and faculty administration
  • Lead and contribute to the operation and development of geochemistry research laboratories
  • Engage in collaborative projects within and outside the University

Qualification requirements

  • A doctoral degree in geochemistry, geology, or a related field
  • Experience in university-level teaching and student supervision is an advantage
  • Research activity in geochemistry and environmental geoscience, commensurate with career stage
  • Excellent proficiency in English, both written and spoken
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with the ability to effectively share and disseminate knowledge
  • Ability to teach in Icelandic is an advantage. The successful candidate is required to attain Icelandic language proficiency at B1 level within four years of appointment. The University of Iceland provides support for acquiring the necessary language skills

In hiring decisions, considerations will be given to how well the applicant fits the current conditions and needs of the Faculty of Earth Sciences.

Application process

The expected starting date is no later than August 1st 2027.

The position will be filled for a term of five years, with the possibility of a permanent appointment thereafter, cf. Paragraph 3, Article 31 of the Rules of the University of Iceland No. 569/2009.

The processing of applications, evaluation of candidates` qualifications, and appointment to the position shall follow the provisions of Act No. 85/2008 on Public Universities and the Rules of the University of Iceland No. 569/2009.

Applicants must submit the following documents with their application:

  1. A cover letter outlining, among other things, how the applicant meets the qualification requirements
  2. Diploma(s) or, where applicable, a certificate of academic record
  3. Academic curriculum vitae (CV)
  4. List of publications
  5. A report on research work and other professional work undertaken
  6. A statement of research and teaching plans if appointed
  7. Information on potential referees who may be contacted

The application must indicate which of the applicant`s publications, up to eight in total, are considered most significant with regard to the position in question. Applicants should submit only these publications with their application or provide information on where they are electronically accessible. If a submitted work has multiple authors, applicants must clarify their individual contribution to the work. Application materials that cannot be submitted electronically should be sent in two copies to the Science and Innovation Department, University of Iceland, Sæmundargata 2, 101 Reykjavík.

All applications will be answered, and applicants will be informed of the outcome once a decision has been made. Applications may remain valid for six months after the application deadline.

In hiring for positions at the University of Iceland, the University`s equality policy is taken into account. Attention is also drawn to the University of Iceland`s language policy.
