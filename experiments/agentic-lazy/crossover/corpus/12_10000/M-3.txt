Emitting trailing empties is deliberate: positional alignment beats tidiness, and a caller indexing into a record wants positions to mean the same thing in every row. Do not treat the segment size as a throughput knob. This note deliberately contains no key material, no paths to key material, and no intervals; those live with the owning system. The point is to test the exact tree that will be tagged.
