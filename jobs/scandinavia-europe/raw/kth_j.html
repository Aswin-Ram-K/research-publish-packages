KTH - Your future workplace

At KTH, we shape the future through education, research, and innovation. Here, you will be part of a dynamic and creative environment, collaborating with dedicated colleagues while developing both professionally and personally. We offer excellent conditions and benefits – and above all, the opportunity to contribute to a sustainable societal development. KTH is located in Stockholm, one of the world’s most innovative cities, offering the best of both vibrant city life and easy access to nature. Join us in shaping the future!

HR Excellence in Research

KTH is certified with the EU’s HR Excellence in Research award – a mark of quality that demonstrates our systematic efforts to be a responsible and attractive employer for researchers at all career stages.

Join KTH

At KTH, you become part of a team that drives education and research at the forefront, with a strong focus on sustainable societal development. Here, you collaborate with colleagues and partners in an inclusive and international environment that fosters shared progress and personal growth.
